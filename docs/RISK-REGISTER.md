# EUA AI Academy — Risk Register

From Programme Design v2.0, Section 20. Review quarterly.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|------|------------|--------|------------|-------|
| 1 | Low completion rates read as programme failure | Certain | High | Set metric before launch: report **absolute completions**; publish drop-off as learning output | Dean |
| 2 | Named tool changes, breaks, or dies mid-year | Certain | Medium | Capability-first curriculum; two-layer video; living tool map with named owner | Curriculum owner |
| 3 | Guardian consent not properly captured | Moderate | High | Required field + confirming email; no account before return; legal sign-off | Legal |
| 4 | Student posts harmful content in Telegram group | Moderate | High | 2 moderators; no DMs; crisis protocol; parents may join | Safeguarding |
| 5 | Telegram group unmoderated or dies | Moderate | Medium | Fixed weekly office hour; moderators seed threads; group health metric | Moderators |
| 6 | Course teaches efficient cheating | Moderate | High | Judgement-weighted exams; student-written charter (L12); integrity spectrum | Curriculum |
| 7 | Students leave cynical rather than critical | Moderate | Medium | False-accusation penalties; challenges where answer is "true, and here is how I know" | Presenter |
| 8 | Exams gamed or shared | High | Low | 40+ item banks; random draw; quarterly corpus refresh; no proctoring pretence | Curriculum |
| 9 | Demo Day attendance decays | Moderate | High | Fixed date never moved; protect open Q&A; rotate challenge finals | Demo Day host |
| 10 | Perceived as competing with national programmes | Low | Medium | Public upstream positioning; FAST referral relationship | Dean |
| 11 | Recorded course ages without refresh | Moderate | High | Layer B annual budget line with named owner | Faculty |
| 12 | Public page overstates current readiness (open enrolment, active certificates, unlimited capacity) | Moderate | High | Pre-launch status; public claim register; comms/legal gate before release | Communications |
| 13 | Mixed Armenian/English copy or inaccessible mobile experience reduces trust and access | Moderate | High | Bilingual terminology owner; caption QA; WCAG review; low-end device testing | QA |
| 14 | Soft launch overlaps year-end holidays and produces misleading results | High | Medium | Re-baseline to January; retain Week 18 change freeze and two calendar-buffer weeks | Programme lead |
| 15 | Remote logo or third-party asset breaks the public page | Moderate | Medium | Host approved brand assets locally; verify social card and metadata in release QA | Web lead |
| 16 | LMS cannot support guardian consent evidence, withdrawal, or certificate verification | Moderate | High | Capability spike in Week 4; no late custom build; narrow scope or change platform early | Technology |

---

## Pre-launch critical risks (Week 1–2)

1. **Consent wording and evidence process unsigned** → blocks personal-data collection and enrolment
2. **LMS capability unproven** → blocks account creation, certificates, withdrawal, and research data
3. **Crisis protocol or moderator coverage missing** → blocks community launch
4. **Public claims not approved** → blocks page and campaign release

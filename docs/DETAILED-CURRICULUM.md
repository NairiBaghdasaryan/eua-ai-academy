# EUA AI Explorers — Detailed Curriculum and Video Runtime Plan

**Version:** 1.1 · 31 August 2026  
**Status:** Proposed for academic, safeguarding, and production approval  
**Audience:** Learners aged 14–16  
**Delivery:** Self-paced, Armenian audio, Armenian captions, English subtitles

This is the production timing master for the 20-lesson curriculum. Video runtime is edited viewing time. Practice begins after a clear **PAUSE & DO** instruction and is not included in the video total.

## Programme load

| Measure | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Recorded video | **8 hr 27 min** |
| Guided practical work | **15 hr 05 min** |
| Core learner time | **23 hr 32 min** |
| Optional module exams | **1 hr 45 min** |

Every module contains **60–88 minutes of recorded video**, meeting the requirement that each module provide one to two hours of video content. Practical work is additional and deliberately longer than passive viewing.

## Module 1 · Foundations — 80 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Distinguish AI from ordinary automation and explain how data, training, and prediction shape model behaviour.

### Lesson 1 · Is That Actually AI? — 20 min video + 30 min practice

- **Outcome:** Correctly classify AI, machine learning, generative AI, and rules-based automation.
- **Video chapters:** 0–6 AI, automation, and machine learning · 6–13 comparing four everyday systems · 13–20 capability and limitation checklist.
- **Practical output:** An Intelligence Audit classifying 10 everyday tools, with one piece of evidence for each decision.
- **Evidence:** Completed audit plus a one-sentence definition of AI in the learner's own words.

### Lesson 2 · How Machines Learn — 20 min video + 40 min practice

- **Outcome:** Describe the data → training → model → prediction chain and explain how data quality changes results.
- **Video chapters:** 0–6 examples, labels, and patterns · 6–13 training versus testing · 13–20 how poor data creates confident errors.
- **Practical output:** A trained three-class classifier and one deliberately discovered failure case.
- **Evidence:** Model screenshot, test log, and explanation of why the failure occurred.

### Lesson 3 · The Prediction Machine — 20 min video + 35 min practice

- **Outcome:** Explain next-token prediction, probability, and the cause of hallucination in plain language.
- **Video chapters:** 0–6 from next word to next token · 6–13 context, probability, and confidence · 13–20 why a convincing answer can be wrong.
- **Practical output:** A two-chat contradiction test with a learner confidence judgement.
- **Evidence:** Paired outputs, highlighted contradiction, and justified confidence label.

### Lesson 4 · The Tool Map — 20 min video + 30 min practice

- **Outcome:** Choose an appropriate AI capability based on the problem, data sensitivity, and required output.
- **Video chapters:** 0–8 the seven-capability AI map · 8–14 matching problems to tools · 14–20 age, privacy, cost, and access constraints.
- **Practical output:** A personal use / do not use / check first map for five real tasks.
- **Evidence:** Five decisions, each with capability, risk, and fallback.

**Module check:** 15-minute optional scenario exam; pass threshold proposed at 70%.

## Module 2 · Communication — 60 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Write precise instructions and use a repeatable workflow in which the first response is always checked and improved.

### Lesson 5 · CRISP: How to Ask — 30 min video + 40 min practice

- **Outcome:** Write a complete instruction using context, role, intent, specifics, and parameters.
- **Video chapters:** 0–10 the five elements of CRISP · 10–20 examples, constraints, and output formats · 20–30 diagnosing and rewriting a weak prompt.
- **Practical output:** Three rewritten prompts and one reusable CRISP template.
- **Evidence:** Before-and-after outputs with a short quality comparison.

### Lesson 6 · The Second-Draft Rule — 30 min video + 40 min practice

- **Outcome:** Critique, verify, and revise an AI first response through at least two deliberate rounds.
- **Video chapters:** 0–10 why the first response is only a draft · 10–21 critique → verify → revise · 21–30 quality criteria and version logs.
- **Practical output:** A complete first-response-to-final-response revision case.
- **Evidence:** Original, critique, verification notes, changes, and final version.

**Module check:** 15-minute optional prompt repair exam; pass threshold proposed at 70%.

## Module 3 · Critical thinking — 88 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Check facts, sources, bias, and data risks before accepting or sharing an AI-generated result.

### Lesson 7 · The Confidence Trap — 22 min video + 35 min practice

- **Outcome:** Recognise fabricated facts and sources without confusing a confident tone with evidence.
- **Video chapters:** 0–7 what hallucination is—and is not · 7–15 fabricated quotations and false sources · 15–22 claims, evidence, and confidence ratings.
- **Practical output:** A claim audit labelling three statements verified, unverified, or disproved.
- **Evidence:** Source links and a confidence reason for every label.

### Lesson 8 · Whose Data? Bias and Blind Spots — 22 min video + 40 min practice

- **Outcome:** Identify representation gaps in data and explain who may be treated unfairly by an output.
- **Video chapters:** 0–7 who appears in training data · 7–15 three sources of bias · 15–22 fairness questions in an Armenian context.
- **Practical output:** A four-prompt comparison and concise bias evidence card.
- **Evidence:** Observed pattern, affected group, missing evidence, and mitigation.

### Lesson 9 · The Reality Test — 22 min video + 45 min practice

- **Outcome:** Apply Question → Verify → Compare → Decide and document the basis for a conclusion.
- **Video chapters:** 0–7 breaking a claim into checkable parts · 7–15 primary and independent sources · 15–22 resolving conflict and logging a decision.
- **Practical output:** A one-page verification report on one viral claim using at least two independent sources.
- **Evidence:** Claim, sources, comparison, decision, confidence, and unresolved uncertainty.

### Lesson 10 · Your Data, Your Rights — 22 min video + 35 min practice

- **Outcome:** Distinguish public, personal, and sensitive data and decide what must never be entered into an AI tool.
- **Video chapters:** 0–7 three levels of personal data · 7–15 consent, retention, and digital traces · 15–22 copyright, attribution, and AI-use disclosure.
- **Practical output:** A privacy settings audit and five family rules for information that must not be entered.
- **Evidence:** Completed audit with one corrective action.

**Module check:** 15-minute evidence triage exam; this is the programme's highest-weight judgement check.

## Module 4 · Learning with AI — 60 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Use AI as a learning partner while protecting academic integrity and the learner's own thinking.

### Lesson 11 · The Tutor in Your Pocket — 30 min video + 45 min practice

- **Outcome:** Design a question-led tutoring dialogue that explains, checks understanding, and adapts difficulty.
- **Video chapters:** 0–10 explaining versus doing the work · 10–20 Socratic questions, hints, and feedback · 20–30 retrieval checks and error logs.
- **Practical output:** A 30-minute tutoring session on a real topic.
- **Evidence:** Session plan, transcript excerpt, and a no-AI explanation showing retained understanding.

### Lesson 12 · Research and the Line You Won't Cross — 30 min video + 45 min practice

- **Outcome:** Distinguish legitimate assistance from cheating and transparently describe AI's contribution.
- **Video chapters:** 0–10 idea, source, draft, and final answer · 10–20 citations, quotations, and reproducible research · 20–30 personal integrity boundary and disclosure format.
- **Practical output:** A five-source mini research pack and signed personal AI-use charter.
- **Evidence:** Search log, citations, AI disclosure, and integrity decisions.

**Module check:** 15-minute academic integrity scenario exam.

## Module 5 · Creation — 75 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Turn an idea into visual, written, audio, and motion storytelling through consistent creative direction and transparent revision.

### Lesson 13 · Seeing Ideas — 25 min video + 50 min practice

- **Outcome:** Direct and iteratively revise a visual concept while controlling composition, style, and purpose.
- **Video chapters:** 0–8 composition, style, and visual hierarchy · 8–17 references, constraints, and variants · 17–25 selecting failures, revising, and crediting.
- **Practical output:** Three versions of *Yerevan in 2075* with documented selection criteria.
- **Evidence:** Prompt or direction log, rejected versions, final visual, and AI disclosure.

### Lesson 14 · Worlds, Words and Voices — 25 min video + 50 min practice

- **Outcome:** Create a short script and audio story while respecting character voice, consent, and copyright boundaries.
- **Video chapters:** 0–8 story arc and character objective · 8–17 voice, music, consent, and imitation · 17–25 human editing and audio quality checks.
- **Practical output:** A 60–90 second audio story with script, source list, and editing log.
- **Evidence:** Script, audio file, credits, and consent/copyright check.

### Lesson 15 · Motion and Message — 25 min video + 60 min practice

- **Outcome:** Design a short video in which every shot serves a defined audience and message.
- **Video chapters:** 0–8 audience, one message, and storyboard · 8–17 shot generation, motion, and transitions · 17–25 misinformation risk, labelling, and release check.
- **Practical output:** A 30–45 second public-interest message with a six-shot storyboard.
- **Evidence:** Storyboard, finished cut, release checklist, and AI label.

**Module check:** 15-minute creative judgement exam based on copyright, consent, and disclosure scenarios.

## Module 6 · Building — 84 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Draw a defensible conclusion from data and build, test, and present a small working no-code product.

### Lesson 16 · Data Detective — 28 min video + 55 min practice

- **Outcome:** Clean, question, and visualise a small dataset without confusing correlation with causation.
- **Video chapters:** 0–9 table structure and data quality · 9–19 questions, formulas, and AI-assisted analysis · 19–28 chart choice, outliers, and causation.
- **Practical output:** One truthful chart and a three-evidence bounded conclusion.
- **Evidence:** Cleaned data, chart, finding, caveat, and reproducible steps.

### Lesson 17 · Build Without Code I — 28 min video + 60 min practice

- **Outcome:** Frame a user problem, define a minimum viable product, and build a functional first version.
- **Video chapters:** 0–9 problem, user, and success measure · 9–19 input → logic → output · 19–28 rapid prototype and functional check.
- **Practical output:** v1 of the Revision Router study tool demonstrated with three realistic inputs.
- **Evidence:** Problem statement, flow, working link or recording, and test results.

### Lesson 18 · Build Without Code II: Test, Break, Ship — 28 min video + 65 min practice

- **Outcome:** Write tests, find failures, improve user experience, and safely present a final product.
- **Video chapters:** 0–9 edge cases and misuse tests · 9–19 user experience, accessibility, and errors · 19–28 safe release, demonstration, and retrospective.
- **Practical output:** A tested v2 and 90-second demonstration.
- **Evidence:** Ten-case test log, at least three fixes, accessibility check, and release note.

**Module check:** 15-minute product judgement exam based on data and testing scenarios.

## Module 7 · What next — 60 minutes of video

**Module objective:** Understand an agent workflow and AI's effect on careers, then defend a responsible capstone proposal.

### Lesson 19 · From Chatbot to Agent — 30 min video + 45 min practice

- **Outcome:** Distinguish an assistant from an agent and map a supervised Input → AI → Decision → Action workflow.
- **Video chapters:** 0–10 answering assistant versus acting agent · 10–20 tools, memory, boundaries, and permission · 20–30 human approval, failure risk, and stop points.
- **Practical output:** One useful agent workflow including data, actions, three risks, and mandatory human approvals.
- **Evidence:** Labelled workflow and failure-response plan.

### Lesson 20 · AI and Your Future — 30 min video + 60 min practice

- **Outcome:** Assess AI's effect on a chosen profession and present an evidence-based, responsible capstone proposal.
- **Video chapters:** 0–10 tasks change, not only job titles · 10–20 human capabilities, accountability, and lifelong learning · 20–30 capstone problem, evidence, and impact.
- **Practical output:** A three-minute capstone pitch covering problem, user, AI role, verification, risks, and success measures.
- **Evidence:** Slide or one-page proposal, source list, risk controls, and presentation recording.

**Module check:** 15-minute agent and future-of-work scenario exam; capstone proceeds to Demo Day review.

## Production and approval rules

1. The runtime shown for each lesson is a content budget, not permission to add filler. A final master may vary by ±2 minutes only if the module total remains between 60 and 120 minutes and the curriculum owner signs off.
2. Practice time is always displayed separately. A silent pause card states the expected practice duration; the full waiting time is not embedded in the video.
3. Every chapter needs an Armenian caption file, an English subtitle file, a transcript, and a chapter marker in the LMS.
4. Every lesson must produce the named evidence artefact. Completion is based on the artefact, not merely reaching the end of the video.
5. Tool demonstrations are date-stamped and replaceable; concepts and outcomes stay capability-based.
6. Any tool used by a learner must pass the age, privacy, access, and terms-of-service review before recording.

## Quality gate before filming all 20 lessons

Pilot Lessons **1, 7, 13, and 17** with at least eight representative learners. Approve the format only if:

- at least 80% can state the lesson outcome without reading it;
- at least 75% complete the practical output within the planned time;
- captions, audio, and chapter navigation pass accessibility review;
- no learner is required to falsify age, bypass consent, or expose personal data;
- observed video completion and practice completion are measured separately.

Once the pilot is approved, this document becomes the timing baseline for scripts, storyboards, filming, editing, LMS setup, and the public curriculum page.

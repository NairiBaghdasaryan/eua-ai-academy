---

## What this is

**EUA AI Explorers** is a proposed free, self-paced, university-run video course for **14–16-year-olds in Armenia**. The launch scope includes twenty lessons, seven modules, **8 hours 27 minutes of video**, 15 hours 05 minutes of guided practice, a draft optional-exam and badge model, monthly Demo Days, and a moderated learner community. Every module contains 60–88 minutes of video. Registration, certification, and community access are not yet active.

**Tagline:** *Understand it. Question it. Build with it.*

---

## v2.0 model change

| | v1.0 (cohort) | v2.0 (self-paced) |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | 20 live hours | 20 recorded lessons |
| Enrolment | 12–15, selective | Proposed broad access; capacity tied to funded platform and support |
| Staffing | 2 instructors/cohort | 1 presenter + 2 moderators |
| Demo Day | Final class hour | Monthly public event |
| Reach | ~45/year | Thousands |

**What we gain:** Reach, regional equity (Kapan, Ijevan = Yerevan), near-zero marginal cost, free-at-point-of-use as public good.

**What we lose (honest):** Lower completion rates, no individual instructor attention, weaker unverified assessment, self-reported workbook.

## Success metrics (set before launch)

- **Primary:** Lesson 1 activation, absolute completions, and completion rate reported together
- Lesson 1 within 48h of enrolment (>60% target)
- Drop-off by lesson (curriculum improvement)
- Pre/post learning gains (publishable research output)
- Demo Day attendance

---

## National positioning

Complementary to FAST **Generation AI**, which currently begins in Grade 10. EUA's proposed offer is earlier-stage and broadly accessible. Do not claim a formal feeder relationship without FAST's agreement.

# Soft Launch Playbook — Weeks 19–20 (4–17 January 2027)

**Goal:** 50–100 friendly enrolments; fix drop-off before public launch.

---

## Invitations

- [ ] Pilot families (8 students + siblings)
- [ ] 2–3 partner schools (one teacher distributes link)
- [ ] EUA staff children / faculty network
- [ ] **Do not** run paid ads yet

---

## Funnel metrics (track daily)

| Stage | Metric | Target |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Enrol | Form completions | 50–100 |
| Start | Lesson 1 within **48 hours** | **>60%** |
| Cliff 1 | Reach Lesson 7 (Module 3) | Track % |
| Complete | Lesson 20 | Track absolute count |
| Certify | Any exam sat | Track % |

**Report absolute numbers and percentages together** so leadership sees both reach and funnel quality.

Example: *"87 started; 52 opened Lesson 1 within 48h; 31 reached Module 3; 12 finished."*

---

## Drop-off response

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Cliff at one lesson | Re-shoot Layer B; check tool broken |
| Low Lesson 1 in 48h | Fix welcome email; shorten path to first challenge |
| Silent Telegram | Moderator seeds bad attempt on purpose |
| Exam pass rate ~100% | Refresh judgement items from corpus |

---

## Weekly email (during soft launch)

Send **one specific thing**, never "continue your course":

- Week 19: "This week's Human vs AI challenge — try it before Friday"
- Week 20: "Demo Day date announced — finish Module 3 to enter the Detective final"

---

## QA checklist before Week 21

- [ ] All tools in Tier 1 tested from Armenia
- [ ] Guardian consent flow tested end-to-end with approved test identities and evidence records
- [ ] Mobile lesson playback tested on phone
- [ ] Landing page enrol CTA connected to the legally approved LMS flow
- [ ] Crisis protocol distributed to moderators
- [ ] Drop-off report shared with supervisor

---

## Soft launch exit criteria

Proceed to public launch when:

1. Required consent flow completion ≥85% among invited learners
2. Lesson 1 started within 48 hours by >60% of learners with access
3. No severity-1 or unresolved severity-2 defect in registration, consent, account access, or Lesson 1
4. Fewer than 5% report a blocking language, device, or access issue
5. Support acknowledgement is under one business day
6. At least 3 learner artefacts have documented permission for showcase use
7. Community opens only if two moderators, the incident playbook, and one active challenge thread are ready
8. Legal, safeguarding, curriculum, technology, communications, and sponsor owners sign Gate G5

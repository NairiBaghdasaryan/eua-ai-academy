# EUA AI Academy — Re-baselined Launch Timeline

**Kickoff:** 31 August 2026  
**Public launch target:** 21 January 2027  
**First Demo Day target:** 28 January 2027

> The original 20-week sequence placed the soft launch across the year-end holidays and could not place the first Demo Day on the last Thursday of January. The approved planning baseline should therefore be **20 active delivery weeks plus 2 calendar-buffer weeks**. See the full [Execution Strategy](./EXECUTION-STRATEGY.md).

## Non-negotiable dependencies

1. Legal consent and privacy approval before any learner data collection.
2. Pilot approval before batch recording.
3. Production completion before final LMS QA.
4. End-to-end consent, access, exam, certificate, support, withdrawal, and deletion tests before soft launch.
5. Soft-launch exit criteria before public claims or campaign activation.

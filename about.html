About EUA AI Academy

Helping Armenia move into the AI era - with knowledge, ethics, and purpose.

We believe AI is a powerful general-purpose tool that will transform how people learn, work, create, manage, and serve society. That transformation should be led responsibly, with informed human judgment at its center.

What we believe

AI should expand human capability - not replace responsibility.

Successful adoption is not simply access to the newest tools. It requires knowledge, critical thinking, ethical practice, data awareness, safety, and the confidence to know when human review matters most.

01Our mission

Make responsible AI capability practical and accessible across Armenia.

We equip students, educators, professionals, leaders, and institutions with the knowledge and applied skills to use AI confidently, critically, ethically, and safely. Our programmes connect global advances with Armenia's real educational, economic, and public-service needs.

02Our vision

An Armenia where AI strengthens human judgment, opportunity, and progress.

We envision Armenia becoming a regional example of high-standard AI education and adoption - a place where people and institutions use advanced technology professionally, create local value, and earn trust through responsible practice.

How we work

A transition guided by high standards.

EUA AI Academy brings together academic discipline, industry practice, and responsible technology principles. Every programme is designed around useful outcomes and clear human accountability.

Our team
